# Direct3D through the HAL on Carrizo: V9XD32 passes all but blending and ARGB1555 at 16 and 32 bpp

Date: 2026-10-28

Status: **measured, one machine, three V9XD32 runs on one boot.**
M-XP7053's Radeon R7E (`1002:9874`), boots 20 and 21, an uncommitted ati
build on 94be3984. This is Phase 5 step 1 of
[the 3D plan](../plans/ati-carrizo-gfx8-3d.md). The captures are in
[`../probe/m-xp7053-carrizo-d3d-hal-2026-10-28/`](../probe/m-xp7053-carrizo-d3d-hal-2026-10-28/).

## What was built

The split is Cedar's (`eg_cp.c`, `d3d_eg_map.c`, `d3d_eg.c`).

- **`engines\vi_cp.c`** brings the graphics CP up at the first Direct3D
  batch and leaves it running. It follows V9XCZ3D's measured sequence:
  1. gpu_init's 224 writes (`vi_gpu_init.c`), written directly from the
     HAL;
  2. rlc_resume;
  3. the PFP, CE and ME microcode, now in the tree as
     `vi_gfx_microcode.c`, generated by `gen-carrizo-gfx-microcode.ps1`
     from the linux-firmware images the probes loaded, hashes checked
     and versions 9Ch, E1h, 81h and 97h matching. LICENSE.amdgpu is in
     NOTICES.TXT;
  4. the ring;
  5. the ring test, then the clear state.

  Layout:
  - the ring, the shader slots and the vertex arena share SDMA's 4 MiB
    window: the ring at 64 KiB, the shaders at 84 KiB, the arena in the
    top 2 MiB;
  - every batch ends with the TL draw's end-of-pipe fence;
  - idle is the last sequence number landing.

  A failed step halts the CP and the RLC and writes `V9XCZCP.TXT`; none
  was written.
- **`eng_vi.c`.** Its `wait_idle` also drains the CP, and it drains the CP
  before each fill or copy. `dma_settle` waits for SDMA alone, so 3D
  batches do not wait for one another.
  - A Z16 fill of the whole surface also covers the padded tile rows.
  - A partial Z16 fill is declined: on a 1D-tiled buffer, neither SDMA's
    linear rows nor the CPU would write the right samples.
- **`d3d_vi_map.c`**, pure and host-tested (`test_d3d_vi_map.c`, written
  first), and **`d3d_vi.c`**. The map accepts:
  - 16 and 32 bpp targets, with alpha writes off on XRGB;
  - Z16 with every function, its own pitch, rows padded to 8;
  - RGB565 and ARGB8888 textures, point or bilinear, wrap, mirror or
    clamp;
  - COPY, DECAL, MODULATE and MODULATEALPHA;
  - specular.

  Blending, the alpha test, fog, colour keys and mip filters are refused
  and counted, and the caps do not offer them.
- **The builder (`vi_3d.c`, `vi_shader.c`).**
  - The colour path now keeps memory byte order, as Cedar's does. The VS
    takes a D3DCOLOR's B, G, R, A bytes as x, y, z, w; the T# selects X,
    Y, Z, W; nothing swizzles.
  - New fields: the texture format, a target mask, Direct3D pixel centres
    (`PA_SU_VTX_CNTL` PIX_CENTER 0), the depth pitch and height, and the
    texture address mode.
  - The VS exports the specular as parameter 2. One PS builder makes
    every pixel shader, each with or without specular.
- **The 16-bit side.** `carrizo_hw16.c` stamps `CAP_D3D` when the
  mini-VDD maps the whole window, and selection takes `ATI_VI` with it to
  the new engine.
- **The probe.** V9XCZ3D's Phase 4 draws pass in memory order through
  the changed builder:
  - Gouraud's centre reads `FF818040`;
  - texel `7E57AB1E` lands as `7E57AB1E`;
  - depth and modulate are exact.

  The probe's struct now starts zeroed: the first run after the new
  fields had stack garbage in them and refused or drew nothing.

## What ran

**Boot 20: nothing drew.** Every pixel V9XD32 read back was 0, and every
call returned success. The trace showed `M64Policy07=271`: every draw had
been refused for specular. DirectX 5 and 6 enable `SPECULARENABLE` by
default, so almost every draw arrives with it on. Accepting it and
ignoring it would be wrong for any non-black specular, so the specular
path was built. That needed new shaders and nothing else.

**Boot 21:**

| Test | 32 bpp (two runs) | 16 bpp |
|---|---|---|
| T1 flat | 0 mismatches, `0013579B` | 0 mismatches, `12B3` |
| T2 Gouraud corners | 7 of 7 | 7 of 7 |
| T3 depth | pass (7) | pass |
| T4 offscreen target | 0 mismatches, `002468AC` | 0 mismatches |
| T5 precision | pass | pass |
| T6 ARGB1555 texture | **fail**: not offered | **fail**, the same |
| T7 SRCALPHA blend | **fail**: blending refused | **fail**, the same |
| T8 sloped depth | pass (F) | pass |
| T9, T10 | pass (F, 3F) | pass |
| T11 culling, six submit forms | all `3F` | all `3F` |
| T12 two stages | skipped: one stage offered | skipped |
| T13 volume, T14 child, T15 memory | PASS | PASS |

`Failures=2` and `Result=FAIL` in every run, from T6 and T7 alone.

The trace after the three runs:
- 4,767 engine batches, 278,508 triangles;
- `EngineIdleTimeouts=0`;
- `BatchesEngineRefused=6`: 3 blend (`M64Policy04`) and 3 texture format
  (`M64Policy09`), one of each per run;
- `V9XHW.INI` reads `Direct3D=hardware-vi-cp`.

The desktop drew normally afterwards.

## What the evidence says

- **The CP runs inside the HAL as it ran in the probe.** It started at
  the first batch, with no quarantine, no fault trace and no timeouts over
  three runs and a mode change between them (32, 16, 32 bpp).
- **The probe's sequence carried over unchanged.** That includes
  gpu_init from the HAL and the microcode from the tree.
- **Memory-order colour is right in the real driver at both depths.**
  T1's flat colour and T4's offscreen pixel are exact at 32 bpp, and
  T1's is exact at 16 bpp too. COLOR_5_6_5 with x in the low field gives
  D3D's RGB565, as on Cedar.
- **Direct3D's pixel centres line up.** PIX_CENTER 0 with the core moving
  nothing passes T2's corners, T10's guard band and T13's centres.

## Not checked

- Blending, the alpha test, fog, colour keys, mip chains, and ARGB1555
  and 4444 textures: steps 2 and 3.
- V9XDDP's 3D checks, a game, and 3DMark.
- A partial Z16 clear, which is declined. A game that clears only part of
  its Z buffer will see stale depth there.
- The V9XCZ3D probes since this boot: they reprogram the CP the HAL now
  owns.
- The packages. Every family carries the same V9XHAL.DLL, and the
  54 KiB of graphics microcode puts the S3 family's first floppy
  22,770 bytes over its 1,457,664, so `run-checks` fails at the floppy
  step. Nothing is committed until that is settled.
