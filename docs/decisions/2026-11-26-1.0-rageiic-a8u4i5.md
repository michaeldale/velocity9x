# Rage IIC on the 0.61.0 tree: probes pass but V9XD32's off-screen targets; 3DMark 2000 stops in a depth-fill loop

Date: 2026-11-26. Machine: A8U4I5, the third card of the 1.0 round: Rage
IIC AGP (`1002:4757`, subsystem `1002:4757`, 4 MiB), already bound to
its Velocity9x key (`Display\0006`), `BitsPerPixel` 16. Build: the
`ati` package of `6244dc08-dirty` (the 0.61.0 tree), installed by
`run-release-checks.ps1 -Machine rageiic` (boot 703 to 704, 8 of 8
files hash-checked), desktop 800x600x16.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| V9XGLP, V9XGLIDP, V9XWPRS, V9XFBCMP | PASS |
| V9XR3DP | COMPLETE |
| V9XD32 `/bpp16` | FAIL: T4 (flat triangle into an off-screen target; its clear `E_NOTIMPL`, 990 of 3,861 pixels wrong) and T16 (DirectX 7 device on an off-screen target: every pixel 0, every call `DD_OK`) |
| 3DMark 2000, 640x480x16 by hand | stopped after 1,340 flips in a loop of depth fills; killed |

T20 and T22 count only where the HAL advertises their states and
formats; here `T20Ok=F2F3` and `T22` 0 were not counted. Neither V9XD32
nor 3DMark 2000 had run on this card before, so none of this is a
comparison.

**3DMark 2000.** Set in its Display and CPU Settings to 640x480, 16-bit
colour, textures and Z, triple buffer (2,400 KB of 4,096 used). It drew
for about 4 minutes, then stopped presenting: over 80 s, three V9XTRACE
snapshots show no flip or draw, while `CountBlt` rose by 28,040 and the
trace ring held only `Blt` with `DDBLT_DEPTHFILL` returning handled. The
screen was black with one white streak; 3DMark had one thread; Windows
and the agent stayed up, and killing 3DMark gave back the desktop.
`M64Refused` rose 7 to 7,076 (last reason texture format) before the
stop. Which test it was in is not known.

Filed: [issue](../issues/2026-11-26-rage-iic-3dmark2000-depth-fill-loop-and-offscreen-targets.md).

Not done: games, the DOS box, Stand By.

Evidence: [`../probe/rageiic-1.0-2026-11-26/`](../probe/rageiic-1.0-2026-11-26/)
(`release-checks.md`, `rageiic/`, `3dmark-640-stall/` with the
snapshots before and during the stall, `V9XTRACE.INI` and the screen).

## 2026-11-26, later: Half-Life and 3DMark 99, which ran in October

Michael asked for a program that worked on this card in an older
release.

- **Half-Life Direct3D** (`hl.exe -d3d -w 640 -h 480 -full -console
  -condebug +timedemo mwd5`, the October command, which ran at
  4.29 fps on our driver,
  [record](2026-10-03-rage-iic-half-life-d3d-against-ati.md)): the
  machine locked during the demo; the console log ends at "Playing
  demo"; Michael power-cycled it (boot 704 to 705). No fault record was
  written. **A regression from October.**
- **3DMark 99 Max**, its default 800x600x16, Z16, triple buffer: 24 of
  26 tests ran with nothing refused and no engine timeout (Trilinear
  Filtering drawn correctly), then at Image Quality 1 it stopped in
  the same loop as 3DMark 2000: `FlipHandled` and `M64Draws` still at
  1,730 and 146,731 for 24 s while the trace ring filled with
  `DDBLT_DEPTHFILL` Blts. Killed. In October 3DMark 99 was judged
  "looking good" by eye but its score was never read, so whether Image
  Quality finished then is not known.

Evidence: `../probe/rageiic-1.0-2026-11-26/half-life-d3d/` and
`3dmark99/`.
