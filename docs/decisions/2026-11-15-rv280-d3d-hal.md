# RV280 Direct3D HAL: V9XD32 passes at 16 and 32 bpp

Date: 2026-11-15

Machine: M-HNBJNV, Radeon 9250 (`1002:5960`), Windows 98 SE, Pentium III
1 GHz on a VIA PM133. Driver: branch `ati-rv280`'s ati package, built
dirty on `fed3d8f0`, boots 20 to 22. Probes: `V9XD32` (defaults, then
`/bpp16`) and `V9XGLP`. Phase 5 of
[`../plans/ati-rv280-radeon-9250.md`](../plans/ati-rv280-radeon-9250.md),
after the [cull, perspective and fog probe](2026-11-15-rv280-cull-rhw-fog-probe.md).
Evidence: [`../probe/field-radeon-9250-hnbjnv-2026-11-15/`](../probe/field-radeon-9250-hnbjnv-2026-11-15/),
`d3d-hal-boot20/` to `d3d-hal-boot22/`.

## What was built

The RS690's split:
- **The map:** `d3d_rv280_map.c` turns the neutral draw into the
  builder's state. Its host test (`test_d3d_rv280_map.c`) failed against
  a stub first.
- **The engine:** `d3d_rv280.c` places textures and Z, then writes state
  and TL vertices by PIO. Each `SE_VF_CNTL` carries four triangles, the
  most the probes drew in one walk.
- **The 2D engine module:** `eng_rv280.c` writes the R200 3D init at
  validation. Its new `v9x_rv280_3d_start` waits out pending 2D work
  before a draw.
- **Selection:** `CAP_D3D` and `hardware-rv280-pio` on the row,
  `V9X_D3D_SELECT_RV280` (20), and render-interface ABI engine 17.

**Depth.** R200 depth is always tiled, so `depth_tiled` is 1.
- A depth fill of the whole surface goes to the 2D engine. It is widened
  to the pitch and to rows padded to 32
  (`V9X_RV280_DEPTH_ROW_ALIGN`), as `eng_r6.c` does.
- A partial depth fill is declined, which fails it.
- The 32-row padding is a margin, not a measured tile height.
- A draw with Z flushes the Z cache in its finish, so a later 2D fill is
  not overwritten.

## Boot 20: 28 failures at 32 bpp, 5 at 16 bpp

`Failures` is a count, not a mask.

- **The X byte (32 bpp only).** T1's inside pixels read `FF13579B` for
  `0013579B`, every one of the 990 judged (`T1InsideFirst`'s low half
  `579B` is the right colour). `RB3D_PLANEMASK` `00FFFFFF` was written
  but did nothing. Mesa's `r200ColorMask` sets `PLANE_MASK_ENABLE`
  (`RB3D_CNTL` bit 1) whenever a channel is masked
  (`r200_state.c:685-698`). The builder now does the same; its host check
  failed first. This one cause accounts for the extra 32 bpp failures
  (T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T8, T13, T14).
- **Formats.** T6, T16 and T20's alpha-test quadrants use ARGB1555, and
  T22X uses XRGB8888. The engine offered only RGB565 and ARGB8888, so
  those draws were refused and drew nothing.
  - Added from `r200_reg.h`: ARGB1555 (3) and ARGB4444 (5) with
    `ALPHA_IN_MAP`, and XRGB8888 as ARGB8888 without it.
  - The builder's texture gains a `format` field. `DEFAULT` keeps the
    measured RGB565 / ARGB8888 meaning.
- **Mipmaps.** T21 drew level 0 at every size: the caps claimed mip
  filters, but only level 0 is sampled. The mip filter caps are
  withdrawn, so T21 now skips (`SKIP-NO-MIP-CAPS`). A mip filter that
  arrives anyway is drawn with its level filter.

At 16 bpp, everything not in those groups passed on boot 20:
- T1, T4 and T14 flat: `EXACT`;
- Gouraud corners, Z (T3, T5, T8), culling (T9, T11), the T13 volume;
- ARGB8888 textures (T22A), blending (T7), and vertex fog (T20 bits 0-1).

## Boot 21: V9XD32 passes; OpenGL drew nothing

V9XD32: `Failures=0`, `Result=PASS` at both depths.
- T6, T16 and T22X are `0F`: ARGB1555 and XRGB8888 sample right, at 16
  and 32 bpp. So does ARGB4444's register code, as far as no test uses
  it.
- T1 reads `0013579B`.

V9XGLP returned `Result=PASS`, but every geometry pixel read 0:
- `GeometryLeftPixel`, the texture pixels, offset, fog, CVA and TnL all
  read 0;
- clears and the scissor were right.

The cause:
- The render interface's draws are explicit (scissor, write mask). The
  map refuses those.
- A refused interface draw goes to the software engine only for engines
  in `v9x_r3d_fallback` (i9xx, MGA, Rage 128), whose linear Z16 the CPU
  can share. The RV280's Z is tiled, so it cannot be added there.
- Every OpenGL draw was dropped. Before this engine existed (boot 19),
  there was no render-interface engine, and OpenGL went its own way.

## Boot 22: the engine stays out of the render interface

`V9X_D3D_ENGINE_LIMITS` gains `render_interface_off`, appended. With it
set, `v9x_r3d_engine()` answers none, and the interface's entry points
answer `NOT_READY`, as on a chip with no Direct3D engine. The RV280 sets
it.

- **V9XD32:** `Failures=0`, `Result=PASS` at 32 and 16 bpp.
- **V9XGLP:** `Result=PASS`, and the draws land:
  - `GeometryLeftPixel=0000FF00`, `TextureRightPixel=00FF0000`;
  - `LitResult=match`, `TnlResult=match` (28195 pixels drawn).

  `Renderer=GDI Generic`: with no engine our ICD steps aside, and
  Windows' own software OpenGL draws. `CvaResult=FAIL-MISSING` is that
  renderer lacking the extension.

## Not checked

- 3DMark 2000 and games. Only the probes ran.
- A partial depth clear. The core fails it here. V9XD32's clears are
  whole-surface.
- The Z buffer's tile layout, and whether 32 rows cover it at every
  height. V9XD32's surfaces are 640x480, a multiple of 32 rows. This is
  plausible, not proven.
- ARGB4444 on the card.
- Throughput of PIO on this machine.
- Glide and 3DCIF. They take the same `NOT_READY` path as OpenGL, which
  was not run.

## Next

- OpenGL on the R200 needs the interface's scissor and write mask: a
  probe of `RE_TOP_LEFT` / `RE_WIDTH_HEIGHT` as a scissor, and the
  plane mask measured above. Then `render_interface_off` comes off.
- Mipmaps, the colour key, and partial depth fills follow.
