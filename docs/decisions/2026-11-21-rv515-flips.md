# RV515 flips through D1GRPH: every flip taken on M-5CAPM3

Date: 2026-11-21
Machine: remote-help `M-5CAPM3`, Radeon X1300 `1002:7146`, boot 39,
800x600x16.
Build: ati `21e0358f` (branch `ati-rv515`), DRV, VXD, HAL, SETP and GL by
WININIT rename over `c4ef4973`; staging folder empty after the reboot,
DRV, VXD and HAL hashes matched. Reports in
[`docs/probe/m-5capm3-rv515-2026-11-21/flips-boot39/`](../probe/m-5capm3-rv515-2026-11-21/flips-boot39/).
Plan: [`ati-rv515-x1300.md`](../plans/ati-rv515-x1300.md), phase 4.

## Change

The RV515 row adds `V9X_DD_ENGINE_CAP_FLIP`, nothing else.
`rs690_scanout.c` is Linux's `rs600_page_flip` (D1GRPH_UPDATE lock, both
surface addresses, flip at vertical blank), and Linux's `radeon_asic.c`
gives the RV515 the same routine (`rv515_asic`, line 832 of the copy in
`build\external\rs690\linux-radeon\`). The flip address is the frame
buffer's GPU base, `D0000000`, plus the surface offset, which is where the
register probe found the VBE desktop.

## Result

- `V9XHW.INI`: `EngineCaps=00000007` (fill, copy, flip).
- `V9XDDP` (`21e0358f`): `Result=COMPLETE`, `FlipHr=0`, 20 flips in 310 ms
  (15.5 ms each, a 60 Hz vertical blank's pace), longest 17 ms. The other
  DirectDraw checks pass as on boot 38. `FlipPixelOk=0`: that key reads
  back through GDI after a real flip and has been a known instrument
  defect since 2026-08-11 (`2026-08-11-directdraw-hal.md`), so it says
  nothing either way.
- `V9XTRACE` (`V9XSNA3.INI`): `FlipHandled=23`, `FlipTakenAtDone=23`
  (the controller had cleared SURFACE_UPDATE_PENDING for every flip the
  driver called done), where boot 38 without the cap had
  `FlipDeclined=23`. No timeouts. `CountBltEngine=7`.
- `V9XRVMM` after the run: D1 scans `D0000000` (FB offset 0) at 800x600,
  16 bpp, no update pending or locked. The desktop came back on screen
  after the flipping program exited.
- `BootCounter` stayed 39.
