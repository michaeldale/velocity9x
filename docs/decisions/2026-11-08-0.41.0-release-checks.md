## Trinity at 16 bpp: OpenGL reads go wrong from the mip sweep on

V9XGLP's `Result=PASS` does not count its drawing sub-checks. On
Trinity, with the desktop at 16 bpp, the checks up to the front-buffer
ones read back what they should. From the SGIS mip sweep on they do
not: `SgisMipOff64=L6` (white) where the R3E reads `L0`, `OffsetNone`,
`CvaLockedDraw` and every `Fog*` read `0x00FFFFFF`, even though each of
those tests clears to black first. `Cva` is the first of them with a
verdict line.

Not caused by 0.41.0's write-combining, the one 0.41.0 change on this
chip:

| Boot | `MtrrWrite` | `SgisMipOff64` | `OffsetNone` | `CvaLockedDraw` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60, release check | `ok` (WC on) | L6 | FFFFFF | FFFFFF |
| 60, rerun | `ok` (WC on) | L6 | FFFFFF | FFFFFF |
| 61, `NOWC.TXT` | `off-marker` | L6 | FFFFFF | FFFFFF |

Trinity has no earlier V9XGLP report with these checks to compare
against; Cedar at 32 bpp passes them (`m-bxmfa8-cedar-z16-rows`, boot
43). Whether 16 bpp is the condition, and which call leaves the state
behind, was not measured. Carried into 0.42.0. Evidence:
`trinity/glp-rerun-boot60-wc-on-V9XGLP.INI`,
`trinity/glp-boot61-wc-off-V9XGLP.INI`,
`trinity/boot61-wc-off-V9XBOOT.INI`.
