# RIVA 128ZX: mipmaps, where the chip reads its levels and how it picks one

Date: 2026-11-04

Machine: M-7V636C (remote help), RIVA 128ZX 8 MiB, Windows 98 first
edition, boots 27-30, desktop 800x600x16 at 5:5:5. Plan:
`docs/plans/nvidia-riva128-d3d.md`, step 5's open list. Files:
`docs/probe/m7v636c-riva128-mip-2026-11-04/` (`run1`-`run3`, the decoder
`mip_decode.py` and its output `decoded.txt`) and
`docs/probe/m7v636c-riva128-games-2026-11-04/b28-b30-*`.

## Why

Every V9XDDP mip check read level 0: the HAL sent one level
(TEXTURE_FORMAT's SIZE_MIN = SIZE_MAX). envytools names SIZE_MIN (27:24),
SIZE_MAX (31:28) and FILTER's SPREAD_X, SPREAD_Y and SIZE_ADJUST, and says
nothing about the layout or the choice.

## The probe: V9XN32D /d3dmip

An 8x8 chain whose every texel names its own storage index (red 2 x
(s & 15), green 2 x (s >> 4), blue 31), so a pixel says which bytes the
chip fetched. Four 32x16 quads, point sampled. Byte-identical on reruns.

Run 1 (cases 41-47), footprints of 1/2, 2, 4 and 8 texels a pixel:
- Level 1 (4x4) is the 16 texels at s 64-79: right after level 0,
  swizzled.
- Unbiased, 1/2 and 2 read level 0, 4 and 8 read data at s 64. Nothing
  below s 64 was ever read, at any SIZE_MIN.
- SIZE_ADJUST 10h moved the switch later, F0h earlier. SPREAD_X/Y (04h,
  1Fh) and bilinear changed nothing.

Run 2 (cases 48-53): footprints up to 128, SIZE_MIN 1, a chain from 4x4,
each axis alone. Still nothing past s 64; the 4x4 chain's level 1 was
never seen; u counts for more than v. High footprints alias too much to
say which level was read.

Run 3 (cases 54-57), magnified (1/2 texel a pixel, so a level shows whole)
with SIZE_ADJUST alone choosing the level:

| SIZE_ADJUST | 8x8 chain reads | 4x4 chain reads |
|---|---|---|
| F0h, E0h | level 0, s 0-63 | level 0, s 0-15 |
| D0h | 4x4 at s 64-79 | level 0 |
| C0h | 2x2 at s 64-67 | 2x2 at s 0-3 |
| B0h to 80h | 2 texels at s 64-65 | - |
| FFh to F8h | level 0 | - |

**Levels under 8x8 overlap.** Level 2 of the 8x8 chain is read at s 64,
not 80, and level 1 of the 4x4 chain at s 0, not 16. Both fit the chip
rounding a level's offset down to 128 bytes: levels of 8x8 and more sit
back to back, and every level after the last 8x8 starts where it ends.
Run 1's "never below level 1" was this: levels 2 and 3 read the same
bytes. The bias is signed, a level per 10h.

## What was built

- `nv3_3d.c`: `texture_levels`; SIZE_MIN = log2 - (levels - 1); a chain
  must stop at 8x8 (`V9X_NV3_MIP_LOG2_MIN`) or is refused;
  `v9x_nv3_3d_chain_bytes` for the copy and the bounds check; FILTER
  carries the bias for a chain and stays 0 for one level. Host tests.
- `d3d_nv3.c`: the swizzled copy holds the chain, level after level, down
  to 8x8 and only while both edges halve. A write to any level marks it
  stale. A mip min filter samples the chain; LINEARMIP* take the nearest
  level. Caps add MIPNEAREST and MIPLINEAR.

## The bias, by V9XDDP

| Boot | SIZE_ADJUST | Ok keys | Mip checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27 | one level | 200 | all fail |
| 28 | F0h | 207 | ChainB, ChainC read level 2 at 2.7 texels a pixel |
| 29 | F8h | 208 | ChainRows read level 0 at 1.7 |
| 30 | F4h | 209 | all level choices pass |

Boot 28 and boot 29 pin the bias between 0.66 and 0.84 of a level if the
low nibble is sixteenths; that F8h behaved unlike both F0h and 00h says
it is a fraction. F4h is the middle. On boot 30 the ladder reads red,
green, blue, magenta (levels 0-3 of 128 to 16), and ChainA-E, ChainRows,
MipLadder, D3DMipmapLevelSelect and MipTriDegraded pass. None of boot 27's
passing keys fails. 592 batches drawn, 7 refused, no engine timeout.

Half-Life on boot 30, windowed as before: the same door view, 304,309
batches drawn, 924 refused (opaque A4R4G4B4 under the alpha test, as on
boot 27), no timeout (`hl-b30-SNAP.INI`). Whether it uses mip chains is
not recorded: the NV3 HAL keeps no mip counter.

## Not done

- D3DTrilinearBlend and MipTriOk: a blend between two levels, which the
  chip does not have. MipTriDegraded, the nearest level, passes.
- Levels under 8x8 are not sampled: a chain to 1x1 stops at 8x8, which is
  sharper than Direct3D when a texture is far away.
- Whether u and v weigh differently in the level choice was seen in run 2
  (MipChainU / MipChainV) and not modelled.
