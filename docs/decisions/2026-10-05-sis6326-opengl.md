# OpenGL on the SiS 6326 engine: explicit draws, scissor, VRAM textures

Date: 2026-10-05. Machine: A8U4I5, SiS 6326 card 2 (rev 0Bh), boots
294-296. Evidence: `docs/probe/a8u4i5-sis6326-opengl-2026-10-05/`. Plan:
[sis-6326-opengl.md](../plans/sis-6326-opengl.md).

## What changed

- **The mapping takes the render interface's explicit draws.**
  - Perspective, blend, alpha test, fog and depth go through as they do
    for Direct3D.
  - The scissor goes to the clip registers, through new scissor fields in
    the builder state, half-open in and inclusive out.
  - Refused: a write mask other than RGB, and CPU texture levels.
- **The describe publishes the SiS.**
  - Engine id 6 and renderer "Velocity9x SiS 6326".
  - Hardware textures with power-of-two edges, 1 to 512, square or not,
    in all three formats. The ICD therefore makes DirectDraw texture
    surfaces, which `resolve_texture` reads as it does Direct3D's.
- **The render interface's depth clear** converts its value through the
  engine's depth-fill rule: halved on the SiS, whose Z test compares 15
  bits.
- **Refusals are counted.** The SiS `accepts` now counts its refusals by
  reason in `m64_accept_policy`, as the Mach64 and Rage IIC do.

All are host-tested where they are pure, test first.

## Measured

- **The scissor (SIS3D `/phase8`, boot 294).**
  - The clip registers came out as `0001802Bh` (top 12, bottom 43) and
    `00014027h` (left 10, right 39).
  - All ten pixels read right: one inside and one outside each edge, plus
    both corners. The edges are top-left inclusive and bottom-right
    exclusive.
  - Until now the clip had only ever been the whole target.
- **V9XGLP (boot 295)** passed with `Renderer=Velocity9x SiS 6326`. Its
  geometry, scissor, perspective-texture and vertex-array pixels are drawn
  by the SiS engine: the software engine is not selected, and the SiS has
  no fallback.
- **Half-Life, OpenGL, mwd5, 640x480 (boots 295 and 296):**
  - 5.97-6.47 fps, every frame as the Direct3D renderer draws it;
  - the render interface refused 13 draws, all reason 2 (partial write
    mask or CPU levels).

  On the CPU engine (boot 292) the same demo ran at 0.80 fps.
- **Quake 2 demo1 `timerefresh`, 640x480 (boot 296):** 7.78-7.82 fps, no
  new refusals.

No run timed out or declined a flip.

## Not established

- What the 13 refused draws were: a partial write mask, or textures the
  ICD kept on the CPU.
- The software fallback for the SiS. It is still out of scope: it needs
  the mixed-frame measurement and a depth scale both engines share.
