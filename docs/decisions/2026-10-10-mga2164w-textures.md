# The MGA-2164W's texture engine, measured: 86Box's register map, with four corrections

Date: 2026-10-10. A8U4I5 (10.0.1.172), boot 385, the Millennium II
MGA-2164W (8 MiB) on Velocity9x `matrox` at 800x600x32. Phase 3 of the
[hardware Direct3D plan](../plans/matrox-mga2164w-hardware-3d.md), for the
16-bit texel formats. Evidence in
`docs/probe/a8u4i5-mga2164w-textures-2026-10-10/` (four rounds and a final
run).

## Why this needed a probe

Matrox's public 2164W and 1064SG specifications omit the texture engine:
no texture register, no texture opcode, no texel format
([extraction](../specifications/mga2164w-3d-engine.md)). The only source
was 86Box's `vid_mga.c`, an emulator - section 10 of the extraction. Every
item below was a hypothesis until this run.

## The instrument

`MGA2D.EXE /tex`: `mga_3d.c` builds a textured trapezoid (TEXTURE_TRAP,
TMR0-TMR8, TEXORG, TEXWIDTH, TEXHEIGHT, TEXCTL, TEXTRANS) and models it;
the probe uploads a texture whose texels are all distinct (red carries s,
green t) into the region's top 256 KiB, draws a rectangle of rows over it
and compares the colour (RGB) with the model. Coordinate cases modulate
by white Gouraud, so each drawn pixel decodes back to the one texel that
renders as it; the report writes each row as `s.t`.

## Confirmed as 86Box has it

- **Opcode 0110 draws a textured trapezoid**, with atype I; no hang, no
  timeout in any run.
- **Registers:** TMR0-TMR8 at 2C00h-2C20h, TEXORG 2C24h, TEXWIDTH 2C28h,
  TEXHEIGHT 2C2Ch, TEXCTL 2C30h, TEXTRANS 2C34h.
- **TMR meaning:** TMR6/7/8 are s, t, q at the left edge; TMR0/2/4 per
  pixel, TMR1/3/5 per row (TexSkew: s gains a texel a row through TMR1).
  s and t are scaled so that 1 << 20 spans the texture; TEXWIDTH and
  TEXHEIGHT carry log2 size in <5:0> and size - 1 in <28:18>
  (TexIdentity at 16 and TexSize32 at 32 texels).
- **Linear coordinates** (TEXCTL.npcen) take the floor: a start half a
  texel in, or 7/8 of one, still shows texel 0 (TexHalfStart,
  TexStart7of8); half a texel a pixel shows each texel twice
  (TexMagnify).
- **Wrap and clamp:** past the edge s wraps (TexWrap, and below zero,
  TexNegative); with clampu it holds the last texel (TexClampU).
- **Pitch:** TEXCTL tpitch <18:16> gives 8 << tpitch texels a row
  (TexPitch64: a 16-texel texture in 64-texel rows).
- **Perspective** (npcen clear): q is 16.16 and divides s and t (q = 1 and
  q = 2 with doubled s, t: the identity).
- **Colour key:** a texel whose bits under TEXTRANS's mask (<31:16>) equal
  its key (<15:0>) is not drawn (TexKey). A zero TEXTRANS therefore keys
  every texel out (TexZeroTrans): the key has no separate enable.
- **Modulate** (TEXCTL.tmodulate): each channel is texel x Gouraud >> 8.
- **1555** (format 2) decodes as 86Box has it.

## Corrected

1. **Channels widen by replication**, not by shifting: a 5-bit channel is
   `c << 3 | c >> 2`, a 6-bit one `c << 2 | c >> 4`. Round 1's modulated
   pixels differed from 86Box's shift by exactly that.
2. **Decal shows the Gouraud colour where the texel's alpha under tamask
   equals takey** (TEXCTL bits 26 and 25), and **565 counts as alpha 0**.
   So 565 decal shows the Gouraud colour unless both tamask and takey are
   set (DecalMask0, DecalMask1Key0, DecalMask1Key1); 86Box shows the
   texel for 565 always. For 1555 the rule is per texel on bit 15
   (Decal15Key0, Decal15Key1, alpha set on odd columns). Every textured
   pixel also stores FCOL's alpha byte at 32 bpp, as Gouraud does.
3. **Format 4 is not 4444.** 86Box's TW12 drew a garbage pattern with a
   four-pixel period in rounds 1 and 2; Matrox's own HAL offers no 4444
   ([baseline](2026-10-10-mga2164w-matrox-hal-baseline.md)). The builder
   refuses it. What format 4 is was not pursued.
4. **TEXORG ignores its low five bits:** 8 and 16 bytes past a boundary
   fetched 4 and 8 texels early; 32 and 64 past were exact. The builder
   requires 32-byte alignment.

And one the model now carries with a stated limit:

5. **Perspective adds about 1/8 of a texel before the floor.** With q
   rising 1/16 a pixel the card chose the next texel whenever s / q came
   within 0.111 to 0.130 of it, and only then; floor(s / q + 1/8) fits
   that ramp, and the same ramp on a 32-texel texture, exactly - the bias
   is per texel. A steeper ramp (q from 1 to 3, a quarter-texel start)
   differs at 2 pixels a row, both where s / q + 1/8 is exactly an
   integer: there the card's t rounded up to it and its s fell short of
   it at the same q. The divider is not modelled at exact boundaries;
   the case is reported as `KNOWN-DIVERGENCE`. The error is a texel at a
   texel boundary, not something a driver can see.

## Not settled here

- The palettised formats (TW8, TW4) and the texture LUT load
  (MACCESS.tlutload): phase 3b.
- Texturing with depth, and onto a 16 bpp target (where shading dithers).
- What the card writes for a texel keyed out under depth: the model skips
  its Z write, as 86Box does; unmeasured.
- Filtering: the 2164W has none to set, as expected; the probe never saw
  anything but point sampling.
