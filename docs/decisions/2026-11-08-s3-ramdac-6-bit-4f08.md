# The S3 RAMDAC has no 8-bit LUT; 4F08h always answers 6

Date: 2026-11-08. Contributed by perchrh, from the measurements behind
[trio64-vbios-tools](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools) issue
[#138](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools/issues/138). Facts
about the stock BIOSes, the chips and 86Box only.

## Summary

The Trio64's RAMDAC has no 8-bit lookup table: 4F08h answers 6 bits per
primary whatever the caller asks, and nothing in the hardware changes. S3's
own Windows 9x driver asks for 8 bits and silently ends up with 6. A driver
planning to widen its palette through 4F08h should read back what was
granted - and on S3 parts the answer will always be 6.

## The measurement

From the Trio64 data book (no width bit in SR18, CR55 or the palette ports)
and the emulator run of S3's own Windows 9x driver
([spec-c-vbe, VBE-80](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools/blob/main/docs/cleanroom/spec-c-vbe.md)):
4F08h returns 6 whatever BH asks, and nothing in the hardware changes. S3's
driver asks for 8 bits and ends up with a 6-bit DAC in mode 03h.

## What it means here

The open record
[2026-11-06-vbe-8bit-dac-unused.md](2026-11-06-vbe-8bit-dac-unused.md)
plans to call 4F08h and read back the granted width. On the S3 family the
read-back always says 6, so the plan is right and yields nothing there. No
separate S3 work is owed.

- VBE 1.2 (VS911022), section 6.10 and its note: a width the hardware
