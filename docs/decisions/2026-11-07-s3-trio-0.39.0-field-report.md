# 0.39.0 on three real S3 Trio cards: a contributor's report

Date: 2026-11-07. Reported by perchrh on
[PR 23](https://github.com/michaeldale/velocity9x/pull/23), after the
Trio64V+ Have Disk row was merged for 0.39.0
([2026-11-06-trio64vplus-have-disk.md](2026-11-06-trio64vplus-have-disk.md)).
Evidence, as attached to the PR:
[`../probe/field-s3-trio-0.39.0-perchrh-2026-11-07/`](../probe/field-s3-trio-0.39.0-perchrh-2026-11-07/)
(the three settings reports and the contributor's own record).

This is a field report, not a run of ours. The released 0.39.0 `s3`
package (`186b656c`) was installed by Have Disk on three physical cards.
The evidence is the driver's settings reports plus mode switching and
dxdiag checked by hand. No V9XD32, V9XGLP, V9XGLIDP or V9XR3DP, and no
3DMark.

## Results

| Card | Adapter (settings report) | PCI | Revision (report) | MCLK | Stage | GDI | DirectDraw |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trio64 | S3 Trio32/64 86C764 | 5333:8811 | 00 | 69.800 MHz | enable-ok | PASS 800x600x16 | 7 of 7 |
| Trio64V+ | S3 Trio64V+ 86C765 | 5333:8811 | 00 | 50.113 MHz | enable-ok | PASS 800x600x16 | 7 of 7 |
| Trio64V2/DX | S3 Trio64V2/DX or /GX 86C775/86C785 | 5333:8901 | 06 | 50.113 MHz | enable-ok | PASS 800x600x16 | 7 of 7 |

All three cards:
- 2 MB; the linear aperture is mapped; GDI fill, copy and overlap run on
  the engine.
- DirectDraw fill, copy and page flips on the engine; Direct3D is not
  advertised (`Direct3D=0`).
- Mode switching is live at any depth; dxdiag is clean.
- The runtime mode scan publishes 10, 10 and 27 modes. Modes the BIOS
  does not report are not offered (issue 19's behaviour, as designed).

## Readings

- **The S3 package's first boot on real cards for 0.39.0.** Our 0.39.0
  release checks had no S3 machine. For these three chips, this report
  is what covers that release.
- **The CR2F rule holds on real silicon.** The Trio64V+ is named "S3
  Trio64V+ 86C765". `V9XHW.INI` takes the name from CR2F bit 6
  (`s3_regs16.c`), as the merge corrected it, and the Trio64 beside it
  is named the Trio32/64.
- **The Trio64V+'s "revision 00" is not established.** The report's
  `Revision:` is the PCI revision, which the settings page reads from the
  first `HKLM\Enum\PCI` key whose name matches `VEN_5333&DEV_8811`
  (`v9x_collect_revision`, `settings_status.c`). Windows keeps the key of
  a card that has been removed. If the three cards went through one
  machine, the V+ report may show the earlier Trio64's key, which also
  reads 00. The contributor also says Device Manager showed the 765 name
  on the V+. That fits a real revision of 40h or above, unless the entry
  was picked from the list by hand.
- **If the V+'s PCI revision really is 00,** none of the INF's 29 Trio64V+
  lines (40h to 5Fh) match it. Have Disk would then offer the bare
  `PCI\VEN_5333&DEV_8811` line, "S3 Trio32/64 86C764", which is the same
  install. The card would work, under the older name.
