# HD 2600 XT: Direct3D through the CP, V9XD32 at 16 bpp passes (mips boot 9, fog and key boot 10)

Date: 2026-11-13

Status: **measured, one card, boots 7 to 10.** M-NZHWBR's HD 2600 XT
(`1002:9588`, RV630) on the `ati` driver built from this commit's tree
(dirty against `020dd1c5`), installed by WININIT renames. The Direct3D
step of [the plan](../plans/ati-rv630-hd2600xt.md), after V9XR63D drew
the builder's streams exactly
([record](2026-11-13-rv630-gouraud-texture-depth.md)). Captures:
[`../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/`](../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/).

## What was built

- `engines\r6_cp.c`: the CP in V9XHAL.DLL, V9XR63D's bring-up (microcode
  from `r6_cp_microcode.c`, generated by `gen-r600-microcode.ps1`; ring in
  the EG_MAP window after the DMA ring; `r600_gpu_init`'s table through
  the ring without the two threshold registers), Evergreen's ring, arena
  and drain handling (`eg_cp.c`).
- `d3d\d3d_r6_map.c` (host-tested by `test_d3d_r6_map.c`) and
  `d3d\d3d_r6.c`: Evergreen's mapping and engine on `struct
  v9x_r6_3d_state`.
- `r6_3d.c`: `d3d_order` - the target written SWAP_STD and textures read
  XYZW, so a D3DCOLOR fetched as bytes in memory order is B, G, R, A as on
  Evergreen; RGB565 targets; 565/1555/4444 textures at the 256-byte group
  pitch (128 texels at 16 bits); `texture_opaque` for XRGB8888.
- `r6_shader.c`: the D3D VS (TL vertex, every fetch a mega-fetch, R0
  last) and the NONE/REPLACE/MODULATE PSs with normalised coordinates.
- `eng_r6.c`: waits for the CP before fills and copies and in its idle
  wait; a depth fill short of the whole 1D-tiled Z16 is drawn on the CP.
- The RV630 row stamps `CAP_D3D` (`EngineCaps=00000013`,
  `Direct3D=hardware-r600-cp` in V9XHW.INI); `d3d_select.c` routes engine
  type 15 to the new engine.

So Direct3D's order is right on this card: the 565 and 8888 textures'
colours land in the right channels on a 565 target, and depth through the
HAL agrees with V9XR63D's. The tiled Z buffer reads back FFFFh at the
probe's linear guesses (`ZTiled*`), as it should for a 1D-tiled surface;
T3 and T5 judge depth by the pixels and pass.

T21 fails as expected: no mip chain is placed or sampled; every mip
filter samples level 0. The depth constant of boot 5
([issue](../issues/2026-11-13-rv630-depth-constant-z.md)) did not appear
in either boot.

## Mip chains (boot 9): V9XD32 passes

Chains are now placed in one block in Evergreen's layout
(`v9x_eg_3d_mip_layout`: every level's pitch its width rounded to
MAX(64, 256 / bytes a texel), the levels back to back), which is also
libdrm's `r6_surface_init_linear_aligned` for R6xx, and sampled with
RESOURCE_WORD3 at level 1, WORD5 LAST_LEVEL, SAMPLER_WORD0 MIP_FILTER and
WORD1 MAX_LOD in Mesa r600_state.c's 4.6 fixed point (Evergreen's
measured 4.8 is not R6xx's). V9XD32 /bpp16 on boot 9: T21 levels 0-3
`F800`, `07E0`, `001F`, `FFFF` (red, green, blue, white), `T21Ok=0F`;
every other check as on boot 8; **Failures 0, Result PASS**, no
timeouts. Capture `boot9-V9XD32-bpp16.TXT`.

Only MIPNEAREST was judged (T21's filter). Four quad sizes landing on
four different levels says the LOD scale and the level addresses are
right to the nearest level; trilinear blending is untested.

## Specular, fog and the colour key (boot 10): V9XD32 passes

Evergreen's lit shaders in R6xx forms (`r6_shader.c`,
`v9x_r6_shader_d3d_vs_lit`, `_ps_lit`): the VS fetches the specular
dword as a fourth input; the PS, built per draw into the arena, kills a
keyed texel (R3 = texel - key, the largest |difference|, KILLGT against
a tolerance), modulates, adds specular clamped, and fogs by specular's
alpha, the fog colour and key as ALU literals. R6xx has no interpolation
step, so the PS reads its inputs where the SPI put them (specular in R2
textured, R1 not). The ALU clause's COUNT includes its literal pairs
(Mesa's ndw / 2). Specular, vertex and table fog and TRANSPARENCY are
advertised again (`HalTriRasterCaps=1B0`, `HalTriTextureCaps=0D`).

V9XD32 /bpp16 on boot 10: `T20Ok=FF03`, `T20Expected=FF03` - fog full
and half, the key's four quadrants (the keyed one gone to the background
`8410`, red, blue and white kept), the alpha test and its lightmap pass;
every other check as on boot 9; **Failures 0, Result PASS**, no
timeouts. Capture `boot10-V9XD32-bpp16.TXT`.

Specular is not judged by any V9XD32 check: built and host-tested, not
measured. T20's ADD and DECALALPHA bits are outside its expectation (not
advertised, not built), as on Evergreen.

## 3DMark 2000, flips and the heap (boots 15-17)

3DMark 2000's default run (1024x768x16, triple buffer) first needed the
DMA copy rule ([record](2026-11-13-rv630-dma-copy-alignment.md)); from
boot 15 it runs to its score. Captures in
[`../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/3dmark2000/`](../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/3dmark2000/).

| Boot | Build adds | Score | Flips | Mip chains | Timeouts, refusals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | DMA 8-byte rule | 4465 | none (no FLIP cap); `DrawsIntoPresented` +947k | `MipGapShape` +3,929 | 0, 0 |
| 16 | FLIP on D1GRPH; gap diagnostics | not read | `FlipHandled` +11,365, `FlipTakenAtDone` +11,364; `DrawsIntoPresented` unchanged | `MipTreeDeclined` +6,837, reason 4 (no heap room); `MipGapShape` +2,026 | 0, 0 |
| 17 | 128 MiB heap | 3187 | +11,553, taken +11,552 | declined 0, gaps 0 | 0, 0 |

- **Flips**: `rs690_scanout.c` now serves engine type 15 too - the same
  AVIVO D1 registers and the routine Linux gives `rv6xx_asic`
  (`rs600_page_flip`) - with the address from `eng_r6.c` (the heap's GPU
  address plus the offset). V9XDDP: 20 flips in 332 ms, 17 ms at most,
  one a 60 Hz blank. Its `FlipPixelOk` reads 0, as on every target
  ([issue](../issues/2026-09-02-flippixelok-is-uninterpretable.md)). With
  flips the driver's frame grabs work; two Game 1 frames are kept
  (`boot16-game1-grab*.png`): terrain, fog, alpha-tested trees and the
  blended HUD draw as expected by eye.
- **Mip gaps**: in the VBE's 16 MiB heap a chain often found no single
  block (`V9X_D3D_PLACE_ALLOC`), DirectDraw then placed its levels apart,
  and the chain was drawn from level 0. The row now maps the Evergreen
  rows' 128 MiB (`V9X_CEDAR_MAP_BYTES`; `vram=134217728` in V9XBOOT.INI,
  the card's CONFIG_MEMSIZE is 256 MiB), and boot 17 declines none.
- V9XD32 /bpp16 passes on boots 15, 16 and 17.

The scores are recorded, not compared: boot 17's flips wait for the
blank and boot 15 had none.

## 32 bpp and 3DMark 2001 SE (boot 17)

"The row has no 32 bpp modes", above, was wrong: the manifest's baseline
table lists 8 and 16 bpp, but the driver offers the BIOS's 32 bpp modes
too (V9XDDP's mode list, `boot16-V9XDD.INI`, modes 6-8).

- V9XD32 with no switch (640x480x32): every check as at 16 bpp,
  **Failures 0, Result PASS** (`boot17-V9XD32-bpp32.TXT`). So the
  XRGB8888 target in Direct3D's order is measured.
- 3DMark 2001 SE's benchmark at its saved settings (1024x768x32, 32-bit
  textures, software T&L, low detail): ran to a score, 1376, in 6.3
  minutes; `FlipHandled` +4,936, `FlipTakenAtDone` +4,919; no idle or
  FIFO timeout, no policy refusal, no mip gap; `DrawsIntoPresented` +11.
  Two Car Chase frames are kept
  ([`3dmark2001se/`](../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/3dmark2001se/)) and
  look right by eye. Which of its tests ran was not read (its details
  page); the tests that need features this HAL does not offer are
  skipped by the program.

## OpenGL (boot 18)

The engine now describes itself to the render interface
(`V9X_R3D_ABI_ENGINE_R600`, 16; renderer "Velocity9x Radeon HD 2600 XT";
hardware textures 1 to 2048, powers of two, as Cedar's). Before that the
ICD would have kept every texture on the CPU, which the map refuses.
Captures in [`../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/opengl/`](../probe/rv630-d3d-2026-11-13/opengl/).

- V9XGLP: `Result=PASS`, `LifetimeFailures=0`. `LitResult=differ` (0 of
  40), as on every released engine but the HD 4600 (the GMA 950, GM45,
  R7E and R3E read the same in the 0.47.0 release checks).
- Quake 3 demo 1.11, the owner's settings (640x480 fullscreen, 16-bit
  colour, 16-bit Z), `timedemo 1`, `demo demo001`: **1346 frames, 20.4
  s, 65.9 fps**, twice; `GL_RENDERER` as above; no engine timeout and no
  policy refusal in the trace after it. One windowed frame is kept
  (`boot18-q3-windowed.png`): lightmapped walls, blended decals, flames,
  text and the weapon draw as expected by eye.

V9XTRACE's frame grabs did not fire under the ICD (the files it left
were the 3DMark run's); not looked into.
