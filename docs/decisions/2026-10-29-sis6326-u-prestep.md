# SiS 6326: the driver presteps U, so 1:1 text keeps every column

Date: 2026-10-29

Status: implemented. Measured on A8U4I5 with SiS 6326 card 2 (rev 0Bh) at
boots 650-653, using Half-Life's console under Direct3D and OpenGL. Parked
with the console background still wrong (below).

Evidence: [a8u4i5-sis6326-wc-apps-2026-10-29](../probe/a8u4i5-sis6326-wc-apps-2026-10-29/README.md).
It builds on [2026-10-05-sis6326-3d-textures.md](2026-10-05-sis6326-3d-textures.md),
finding 7: "Texture coordinates, like colour, are not prestepped in x ...
the driver accepts it."

## What was wrong

Michael saw specks beside the glyphs in Half-Life's Direct3D console, and
the same in Quake 2. Under OpenGL every glyph was one texel to the left. "/"
showed as "'", and the L of HALF-LIFE lost its upright.

The engine takes U where a span crosses the triangle's left edge, then steps
it from there. It never moves U on to the first pixel's sample point. So each
pixel reads U off by the distance from that crossing to the sample, times
dU/dx. That distance is between 0 and 1 pixel:

- **Vertical left edge**, the half of a glyph quad left of its diagonal: the
  distance is the same in every row.
- **Diagonal left edge**, the other half: the distance varies row by row.

At one texel per pixel, any error that crosses a texel edge shows as a
column taken from the neighbouring glyph.

## Three attempts, one per boot

| Boot | U correction | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 651 | +1/16 texel, constant | vertical-edge halves right; new specks on the diagonal halves |
| 652 | +½ pixel of dU/dx | samples at texel centres right (OpenGL); Direct3D text, which samples on texel edges, still lost columns |
| 653 | per triangle, below | both renderers whole; their consoles agree pixel for pixel at 8x |

With no correction at all, the Direct3D text in Michael's capture showed
specks but kept most of its glyphs. That was a different error from
OpenGL's whole-glyph shift, because each renderer samples at a different
place within a texel.

## What the driver does now

`v9x_d3d_sis_u_prestep` in `d3d_sis6326_map.c` works per triangle, from the
U plane through its three vertices.

- **One texel a pixel** (dU/dx·width within 1/64 of +1 or −1, and
  dU/dy·width within 1/64 of a whole number). Every sample then sits at the
  same place f within its texel, and the driver computes f.
  - For +1 it moves U so the sample with no error lands 1/16 short of the
    texel's far edge.
  - For −1 it moves U so that sample lands 1/16 past the near edge.
  - Any error below 15/16 of a pixel then reads the same texel Direct3D
    does.
  - A sample within 1/64 below a texel edge is treated as on the edge, as
    Direct3D's floor rule puts it in the texel above.
- **Anything else** gets half a pixel of dU/dx, which centres the error.
  That covers scaled, rotated and perspective triangles, where f differs in
  every pixel and a sub-texel error is not visible.

The gradient is affine, the plane through the vertices as given. Under
perspective it is an average, which is all a per-vertex term can carry. V is
left alone: the engine presteps V, and the 1/256 tie shift already puts V's
samples just inside the texel.

`v9x_d3d_sis_triangle` now takes the bound texture's `log2_width`.
`tests/host/test_d3d_sis6326_map.c` covers:

- two texels a pixel (the centring path);
- one texel a pixel, forwards and mirrored;
- a constant U, unchanged.

Those checks failed on the committed code and pass now.

The HAL links without the C runtime, so the floor uses an inline `fistp`
rather than a cast, which would have pulled in `__CHP`, as `r3d_clip.c`
does. The larger HAL put the Matrox floppy's disk 1 348 bytes over, so
`GLIDE2X.DLL` moved to its third disk, on the grounds `GLIDE3X.DLL` moved
(Michael, 2026-10-28: "third disks for all").

## What the evidence leaves open

- **The console background is wrong under both renderers.** Direct3D draws
  horizontal streaks and OpenGL blocky purple and green noise; neither
  matches the menu's backdrop. Not investigated: parked (Michael,
  2026-10-29).
- **Only the console was checked.** No V9XDDP, 3DMark or Final Reality run
  was made on the boot 653 HAL. Quake 2 was not rerun after the fix.
- **The margins are chosen, not measured:** the 1/16 margin and the 1/64
  snap. The engine's U precision is still unknown.
- **One launch fault.** Half-Life faulted at its launcher splash on boot 652
  (`EIP=00000003`), after the script had closed a run mid-load, and ran on
  the next launch.
