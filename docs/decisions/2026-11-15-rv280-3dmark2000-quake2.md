## 3DMark 2000, default benchmark

Settings: 1024x768, 16-bit colour, 16-bit textures, 16-bit Z, triple
buffered.

| Run | Score |
|---|---|
| 1 | 905 |
| 2, with `V9XTRACE -grab:40x10` | 824 |

Both runs completed to the score window. HAL counters over run 1:
- 915,000 draws and 39.6 million triangles, 817,000 of the draws
  textured;
- no refusals, since the 6 on record are V9XD32's;
- no idle, FIFO or ring timeouts.

Grabs 0, 3, 6 and 9 of run 2 are Game 1's helicopter scene. They show:
- textured terrain and sky;
- alpha-tested trees;
- the blended HUD panels and the rotor blur.

Nothing is missing, miscoloured or depth-wrong in them. Only Game 1 was
grabbed; the other tests' frames were not looked at.

## Quake 2 demo, OpenGL

Command line: `quake2.exe +set vid_ref gl +set vid_fullscreen 0 +set
gl_mode 3`, windowed at 640x480 on the 1024x768x16 desktop.

The console reads:
- `GL_RENDERER: Velocity9x Radeon 9250` and `GL_VERSION: 1.1.0`;
- `GL_EXT_compiled_vertex_array` used;
- `GL_SGIS_multitexture` not found, so lightmaps are a second pass.

The demo has no `demos/demo1.dm2` for a timedemo. `map demo1` loads and
draws the level, with walls lightmapped and the blaster model and HUD
shown.

`timerefresh` (128 frames, a full turn on the spot, bound to F10) took
2.340 s and 2.352 s: **54.7 and 54.4 fps**.

HAL counters over the session:
- 629,000 draws and 22.5 million triangles, nearly all textured;
- no refusals, no timeouts.
