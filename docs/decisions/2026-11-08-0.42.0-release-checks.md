## What changed since 0.41.0

Every probe verdict compared with `0.41.0-release-checks-2026-11-08`, by machine and check.

- **i5-4570, HD 4600**: no verdict changed.
- **Netbook, GMA 950**: no verdict changed.
- **A8U4I5, Rage 128 Pro**:
  - V9XD32 ddi5: FAIL to PASS; T4Flat FAIL to EXACT, T16 FAIL to PASS
  - V9XD32 ddi6: FAIL to PASS; T4Flat FAIL to EXACT, T16 FAIL to PASS
  - V9XD32 default: FAIL to PASS; T4Flat FAIL to EXACT, T16 FAIL to PASS
  - Caused by 0.42.0, as intended. The HAL now places an offscreen
    render target on a 32-byte base. Before, after T20's and T21's
    textures, DirectDraw's heap put it 4 bytes off, and the Rage 128
    refused it
    ([record](2026-11-08-render-target-placement-trinity-rage128.md)).
- **M-JJN1ZZ, Radeon R3E Mullins (remote help)**:
  - V9XR3DP: Clear 0 to 9
  - Not caused by 0.42.0; it was there in 0.41.0. `9` is
    `V9X_R3D_RESULT_TIMEOUT`: the wait for the engine before the CPU
    clear ran out. On the R3E, the first clear of a process sometimes
    times out like this:
    - **0.41.0 release checks (`a5f26008`):** V9XGLP's first `glClear`
      logged `result=9` too. V9XR3DP passed that day.
    - **This morning's run after 3DMark:** V9XR3DP's first clear was 9,
      V9XGLP's was not, and V9XR3DP alone a few minutes later passed
      ([record](2026-11-08-mullins-3dmark2000.md)).
    - **These checks:** both V9XGLP's and V9XR3DP's first clears were 9.

    The engine timeout counters stayed at 0. Every later call in the
    same process worked. The day's V9XGLP lines are in
    `jjn1zz/V9XGL-first-clear-excerpt.LOG`. Shipped as a known issue;
    the cause is open.
- **M-D7TFW4, HD 7660D Trinity (remote help)**:
  - V9XGLP: Cva FAIL to PASS, Tnl differ to match
  - Caused by 0.42.0, as intended. A 16 bpp target an odd number of
    8 pixels wide (the probe's resize to 360) was placed with a pitch the
    target check refused. From then on, every clear and draw was dropped
    ([record](2026-11-08-render-target-placement-trinity-rage128.md)).
- **M-GB8KRE, RS690 (remote help)**: no verdict changed.
