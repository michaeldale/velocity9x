# 3dfx demo6030 on GLIDE3X.DLL: state size, partial downloads, ALPHA_8

Date: 2026-11-21
Machine: remote-help `M-5CAPM3`, Radeon X1300 `1002:7146`, boot 47, ati
`44a4cf1b` with GLIDE3X.DLL from `aec95baa-dirty` (this change) put in
`C:\WINDOWS\SYSTEM` by hand.
Program: 3dfx's "60Hz/30Hz/15Hz demo" (`demo6030.exe`, 1998), Glide 3,
in `C:\Games\3dfx\Demo6030`; the owner's "old glide test tool".

## What stopped it

The census (`V9XGLD3.LOG`, build `e25a096f`) showed the device opening
on the X1300 (`describe result=0`, 640x480, three buffers), then
`grGet(0x06)` unanswered and the process exiting. 0x06 is
GR_GLIDE_STATE_SIZE: the demo sizes a state buffer at start and gives up
when the answer is 0. The wrapper answered neither it nor
GR_GLIDE_VERTEXLAYOUT_SIZE (0x07), and `grGlideGetState`,
`grGlideSetState`, `grGlideGetVertexLayout`, `grGlideSetVertexLayout`,
`grTexDownloadMipMapLevelPartial` and `grLfbReadRegion` were generated
stubs. The demo imports the last two.

## The change

- `grGet` answers 0x06 (200 bytes, `V9X_GLIDE3_SAVED_STATE`: render
  state, vertex layout, coordinate space) and 0x07.
- `grGlideGetState` / `SetState` save and restore that; a restore keeps
  the open window's origin and size. The texture source is not saved.
- `grGlideGet/SetVertexLayout` copy the layout.
- `grTexDownloadMipMapLevelPartial` writes rows of one level through a
  new `v9x_glide_texmem_download_partial` (host-tested). That `data`
  begins at row `start` is from the Glide 3 reference, not measured.
- `grLfbReadRegion` uses the engine's read, as GLIDE2X.DLL does.
- ALPHA_8 textures convert to ARGB4444 with the alpha's top nibble in
  every channel (host-tested). The colour copy follows OpenGlide; what a
  3dfx TMU returns in RGB for ALPHA_8 is not measured.

## On the card

- With the state size answered: the demo runs, 0 refused, but
  `skipped-textured` was about 45% of draws, every skip reason 3 on
  format 2 (ALPHA_8 not converted). The owner, watching, reported it
  "works, it's also missing text", the menu not visible.
- With ALPHA_8 too: `skipped-textured=0`, `refused=0`, 5.2M draws and
  about 56 swaps a second over 75 s. Nobody has seen these frames: the
  agent's screenshot shows only the GDI window, and the owner had
  stepped away. On their return they ran it and reported "Demo 6030
  works as it should now" (06:41Z), menu included.
