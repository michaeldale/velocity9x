# GT218: Z and colour shadows on 64 KiB pages; Quake 3's streaks and top-row garbage

Date: 2026-11-16

Status: fixed and measured on W9QI0DAD (Quake 3 demo, boots 31 and 34);
not yet run on M-BXMFA8, where Daniel reported it.

Evidence: [`../probe/gt218-tiled-64k-2026-11-16/`](../probe/gt218-tiled-64k-2026-11-16/).

## The report

After the OpenGL describe fix ([2026-11-16-gt218-opengl-describe.md](2026-11-16-gt218-opengl-describe.md))
Quake 3 drew, but Daniel (M-BXMFA8, 1024x768, chat 2026-11-15) saw
textures "suddenly corrupt when moving the character", fine again
elsewhere (`m-bxmfa8-q3-owner-0.53.0.png`: floors and walls streaked
along one axis), and in Quake II strips of corruption at the upper-left
and lower-right corners.

## Reproduced on W9QI0DAD

W9QI0DAD had no Quake 3; the demo (`quake3.exe`, `demoq3\pak0.pk3`) was
copied from the Haswell 4570 over the LAN and served to W9QI0DAD by
`http.server`, hash-checked. `demo001` windowed at 640x480, 16 bpp
desktop, F8 (Quake 3's own screenshot, its back buffer through
`glReadPixels`) and the agent's window capture at the same moments.

Both showed the faults: garbage in the frame's first 8 or so rows (white
blocks and coloured noise over the top line of notify text, the same x
positions in every run) and streaked curved walls
(`w9qi0dad-q3-before-4k.png`). Quake 3's own screenshot having them
puts the fault in the drawing, not the present.

## Hypotheses the evidence killed

- **A stale sampler (no `TSC_FLUSH`).** The fixed-function path flushes
  the texture cache but not the sampler cache, as Mesa's
  `nv50_validate_samplers` does. A V9XGLP case (repeating texture drawn
  right after a clamped one, read at u = 1.25) read the repeat colour on
  the unchanged build: `WrapAfterClampOk=1`. The flush was not shipped;
  it stays a difference from Mesa that no measurement has needed.
- **Rendering writing into texture memory in general.** V9XGLP: 48
  textures, 20 depth-tested frames with clears and swaps, each texture
  read back at four points: `SurviveTexturesChanged=0`, frame corners
  the clear colour (`w9qi0dad-boot31-V9XGLP-excerpt.txt`). The probe's
  corners were read at x = 1, outside the band the garbage takes.
- **Lightmaps.** `r_vertexlight 1` (no lightmap pass): the top-row
  garbage in 6 of 6 frames, as before.

## Cause

`create_depth` puts the tiled Z16 and its colour shadow in one block,
Z first, the colour after it, the block on 4 KiB. The engine reaches
them through the alias windows (kind 6Ch for the Z, 70h for the colour,
[2026-11-14-gt218-tiled-alias-window.md](2026-11-14-gt218-tiled-alias-window.md)),
whose page tables map 64 KiB pages. At 640x480 the Z ends 6000h into a
64 KiB page that the colour starts in, and the colour's last page is
shared with whatever DirectDraw places next. The alias-window probe that
licensed this placed its tiled surfaces on 64 KiB; nothing had measured
less, as the constant's comment said ("unmeasured below 64 KiB").

The garbage sits where a shared page would put it: the colour's first
rows, the band after the Z. The textures' streaks fit the other end, a
colour page shared with a texture. That the kind lays out a whole page,
rather than only the surface's block-linear arrangement, is inferred
from this fix working, not measured directly.

## The change

`d3d_nv50.c`: `V9X_D3D_NV50_TILED_ALIGN` 64 KiB; the Z's size and the
colour's each rounded to it, so the Z starts, the colour starts and the
block ends on a 64 KiB boundary of video memory.

The heap cannot be asked for that alignment: `place_chain` asks for rows
as wide as the alignment, and `place_block` refuses an alignment
`fb.linear_base` (a CPU mapping, 4 KiB aligned) does not have. Asked
either way, every Z was declined (boots 32 and 33): Quake 3's
depth-tested draws were all refused (`hw-refused=343441`) and the demo
never left its loading screen. So the block is asked on 4 KiB with
64 KiB to spare and the Z moved up to the next 64 KiB of video memory;
`place_release` still finds the block (the move is under its 64 KiB
window).

## Measured: W9QI0DAD

`top-garbage-counts.txt` counts frames with a run of 20 or more pure
white pixels in the first 12 rows (glyphs are 8 wide, so text never
makes one), with the TGAs' checksums: Quake 3 rewrites its screenshot
numbers from 0, so a TGA a run did not reach is an older one.

| Build | Frames with garbage |
| --- | --- |
| 4 KiB (boot 31), two runs | 13 of 14 |
| 4 KiB, `r_vertexlight 1` | 6 of 6 |
| 64 KiB (boot 34), two runs | 0 of 21 |

The 64 KiB frames show no streaks (`w9qi0dad-q3-after-64k.png`, the
corridor whose floor Daniel's screenshot had streaked;
`w9qi0dad-q3-after-64k-b.png`). Every draw went to the card:
`hw-refused=0/0`, `clear-invalid=0`.
