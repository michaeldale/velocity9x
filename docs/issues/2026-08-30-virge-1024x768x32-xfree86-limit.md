# XFree86 says the ViRGE cannot do 32 bpp at 1024 wide, and this driver ships that mode

Filed: 2026-08-30
Status: open, contradicted by the emulator, unverifiable here
Source: [XFree86 3.3.6 S3V documentation](https://www.xfree86.org/3.3.6/S3V3.html)

## The claim

> 32bpp is limited to a width of < 1024 pixels. (1024x768 is not possible, even
> if you have the memory.) This is a hardware limit of ViRGE chips.

XFree86's `XF86_SVGA` S3V driver programs the ViRGE CRTC directly, so this is a
statement from code that drove the silicon, not a datasheet reading.

## What this driver ships

`packaging\families\s3\family.psd1` declares `1024x768x32` (VBE mode `0118`)
for **both** S3 chips, and README's mode list advertises True Color at 640x480,
800x600 and 1024x768.

## What was measured, and why it does not settle it

`docs\decisions\2026-08-20-high-depth-mode-matrix.md` records 1024x768x32 as
passing on the ViRGE/DX guest and the Trio64 guest, and - importantly - checked
by host-side `PrintWindow` capture of the 86Box window, outside the guest's GDI
entirely: "clean, correct colours and geometry", with `Surface=pitch=4096
dwb=4096 dds=4096 debpp=32`.

That is good evidence against a *driver* fault and no evidence at all against a
*silicon* limit. 86Box need not model a CRTC or streams-processor bandwidth
ceiling that XFree86 worked around by refusing the mode.

Two further reasons the existing evidence cannot close this:

- **This driver does not program the CRTC.** It sets the mode through the VBE
  BIOS, which may configure the part differently from XFree86's own timing
  code - so the limit could be real and still not reached this way.
- **No physical ViRGE exists on this project.** The physical S3 targets are
  Trio64s, and the claim is about ViRGE chips specifically. BARRY refuses
  1024x768x32 anyway, on its 2 MiB, with `DISP_CHANGE_BADMODE` - which is the
  VRAM check working, not evidence about width.

## What would settle it

A physical ViRGE, at 1024x768x32, looked at on a monitor. Nothing short of that
distinguishes "86Box does not model the limit" from "the limit does not exist".

Until then this is recorded rather than acted on. Removing a mode that four
guest runs and a host-side capture say works, on the strength of one sentence
in another driver's documentation, would be trading measured evidence for
documentary evidence - but the documentary evidence is from people who had the
hardware, and a user with a real ViRGE selecting that mode is the person who
would find out.

## Related, from the same page

> Acceleration at 32bpp is limited: only ScreenToScreen bitblit and solid
> rectangles are supported. The ViRGE itself has no support for 32bpp
> acceleration, so the graphics engine is used in 16bpp mode.

This driver declines acceleration above 16 bpp outright - `v9x_virge_fill` and
`v9x_virge_copy` return `V9X_BLT_DECLINED` for `bytes_per_pixel > 2`, and
README lists that as a limitation. XFree86 describes the technique that lifts
it: drive the 2D engine in 16 bpp mode over a surface that is really 32 bpp,
which is the same rectangle at double width. That is a concrete, shipped-driver
precedent for extending fill and screen-copy to 32 bpp, and it is worth its own
work item rather than a footnote here.
